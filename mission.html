Rigid Installation & QA Standards

High-performance security hardware is only as good as the technician who configures it. Falcon Surveillance operates under a zero-compromise installation policy:

  • Premium Conduits: All cables are encased in weatherproof, impact-resistant PVC or steel pipes to prevent weathering or sabotage.
  • Waterproof Junction Boxes: Outdoor connectors are sealed completely inside IP66 junction boxes to block moisture infiltration.
  • Angle Optimization: Technicians run active screen audits to guarantee that lenses are focused perfectly on entry doors, perimeter walls, and high-value zones.
  • App Integration: We configure secure local networks, port-forwarding rules, and app pairings so clients get uninterrupted remote access.
