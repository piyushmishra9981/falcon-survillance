Conventional fire systems group smoke detectors into zones. If a sensor is triggered, the panel shows the zone location (e.g. Second Floor), requiring personnel to search the area manually. For commercial and industrial facilities in Shahdol, Falcon Surveillance installs addressable systems to ensure quick response times.
